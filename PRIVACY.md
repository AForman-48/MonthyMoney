# MonthyMoney Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

MonthyMoney is a personal budgeting app for iPhone and iPad, developed by Alex Forman.

## Your budget

You enter your income, expenses, savings, notes, and optional payment-method labels yourself. MonthyMoney stores this information on your device. It does not connect to your bank, access bank credentials, or process payments.

## iCloud synchronization

When iCloud is available, MonthyMoney uses Apple's CloudKit private database to synchronize budget entries and payment-method labels between your devices. Budget preferences also synchronize using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This information is associated with your Apple Account and is handled by Apple's iCloud services. The developer does not receive or have access to the contents of your private iCloud budget. Without an iCloud account, the app can store your budget locally.

## Tracking and advertising

MonthyMoney does not include advertisements, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics, or tracking. It does not send your budget to the developer's servers.

## Your choices

You can edit or delete budget entries within the app. Settings includes a reset option to clear your budget; deletions may synchronize to other devices using the same iCloud account. You can manage iCloud access and storage through your device's Apple Account settings. Appearance preferences are stored on each device.

## Support

For questions about MonthyMoney or this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/AForman-48/MonthyMoney/issues. GitHub issues are public, so do not include financial information or other private details. Any information you voluntarily post for support is handled through GitHub under its privacy policy.

## Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date.
